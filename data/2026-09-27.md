### [1] [AV-GRPO: Modality-Anchored Decoupling Diffusion Reinforcement Learning for Joint Audio-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29816)
*Zhiyu Xu,Weilong Yan,Yufei Shi,Shiyang Li,Yihao Liu,Kin-Man Lam,Yuewen Cao*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出AV-GRPO（模态锚定的在线扩散强化学习框架）与5DAV数据集，将耦合的音视频联合生成偏好学习解耦为单模态子问题，提升生成质量、语义对齐与跨模态同步。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有音视频联合生成模型存在三个痛点：单模态保真度受限、文本-模态对齐不足、跨模态同步性弱。虽然强化学习后训练有望缓解，但直接迁移到音视频联合生成面临挑战：异构多模态奖励信号纠缠导致信用分配困难；两个模态塔的联合优化因动态差异大而计算昂贵；同步性评估依赖配对样本，难以进行公平的奖励比较。

Method: 提出AV-GRPO（模态锚定的在线扩散RL框架）与5DAV（解耦、难度可控的训练数据集）。AV-GRPO含三个核心模块：(1) 模态锚定的 rollout，用于解耦学习信号并稳定难度；(2) 轨迹锁定+冻结塔的优化，降低开销并重新分配信用；(3) 针对模态特定动态的自适应目标与扰动强度。该设计将耦合的多模态偏好学习转化为单模态子问题，实现精确奖励归因。5DAV数据集从五个维度解耦样本以支持系统化训练。

Result: 在JavisBench与VABench上的实验表明，AV-GRPO在生成质量、语义对齐和跨模态同步方面均优于LTX-2.3，在LoRA与全量微调设置下均成立；消融实验验证了各设计模块的有效性。

Conclusion: 通过将音视频联合生成的耦合偏好学习拆解为单模态子问题，AV-GRPO在保证生成质量的同时改善了跨模态同步，配合5DAV数据集实现了难度可控的系统化训练，为扩散模型的在线RL后训练提供了可行路径。代码与数据已开源。

Abstract: Recent years have witnessed major progress in joint audio-video generation. Existing models still suffer from limited per-modality fidelity, insufficient text-modality alignment and weak cross-modal synchronization. While reinforcement-learning post-training offers a promising remedy, directly adapting it to joint audio-video generation is challenging. Heterogeneous multimodal rewards entangle learning signals and complicate credit assignment. Joint optimization of two modality towers is computationally expensive given their divergent dynamics. Moreover, synchronization evaluation difficulty depends on paired samples, preventing fair reward comparisons. We propose AV-GRPO, a modality-anchored online diffusion RL framework, and 5DAV, a decoupled, difficulty-controllable training dataset. AV-GRPO includes three key modules: (1) modality-anchored rollouts to disentangle learning signals and stabilize difficulty; (2) trajectory-locked frozen-tower optimization to reduce cost and reassign credit; (3) adaptive objectives and perturbation strengths tailored to modality-specific dynamics. This converts coupled multimodal preference learning into unimodal subproblems for precise reward attribution and better synchronization. Our 5DAV dataset decouples samples across five dimensions for systematic training. Experiments on JavisBench and VABench demonstrate AV-GRPO outperforms LTX-2.3 in generation quality, semantic alignment and cross-modal synchronization under LoRA and full fine-tuning. Ablations confirm our designs. Code and data: https://github.com/zhiyuxu03/AV-GRPO

### [2] [WanPE: Towards Cinematic Prompt Enhancement for Modern Text-to-Video Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30221)
*Yubo Zhu,Yawen Shao,Ziyun Dai,Zixun Fang,Kai Zhu,Siyang Sun,Haolan Xue,Chuxin Wang,Tingyu Weng,Jingming Luo,Chen Shi,Lianghua Huang,Yufeng Ai,Yuzheng Wang,Wenyuan Zhang,Yu Shang,Yuxiang Bao,Zoubin Bi,Jie Xiao,Jinbo Xing,Jiaxing Zhao,Chongyang Zhong,Hengjian Chen,Chenwei Xie,Akide Liu,Zhehan Kan,Yu Liu,Wei Zhai,Sheng Zhong,Wei Tong*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: WanPE是一个397B参数的提示增强模型，基于105万真实视频训练，通过镜头级电影化规划与SC-GRPO提升视频生成质量，在30秒场景下人类偏好提升达50.86分。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当代视频生成器已能扩展到30秒并遵循复杂条件，文本提示实际承担了导演职能，需规划动作、镜头、光照与声音的多镜头序列。但用户原始提示往往缺乏电影化规划能力，因此需要专门的提示增强模型来胜任"导演级"规划。

Method: 提出WanPE，397B参数提示增强模型，在1.05M真实视频上训练；通过视频基础的逆向构建（reverse construction）生成镜头级电影化规划，并采用语义一致性GRPO（SC-GRPO）在多镜头与长时程上保持用户需求；同时构建WanPEval人类标注基准，覆盖5-30秒、多种意图粒度，含约1.1万次盲测成对评估。

Result: 驱动Wan3.0视频生成器时，WanPE-397B相比原始用户提示在5-15秒提升人类偏好10.66-18.84分，在30秒场景提升高达50.86分。消融显示逆向构建优于正向重写，SC-GRPO在不同模型规模下均能保持语义保真。在5-15秒区间领先所有评估的商业方案，在30秒区间与Seedance 2.5保持竞争力。

Conclusion: 文本提示已成为视频生成的核心规划接口，WanPE证明导演级提示增强可显著提升长时程、多镜头视频生成的人类偏好，且逆向构建与SC-GRPO是关键有效设计。

Abstract: Video generation begins in text space by authoring a cinematic screenplay, then materializes into pixels. As contemporary video generators scale to 30 seconds and faithfully follow complex conditions, the textual prompt largely directs the production, planning how actions, camera trajectories, lighting, and sound unfold across multi-shot sequences. In this paper, we present WanPE, a 397B-parameter prompt enhancement model trained on 1.05M real-world videos to master director-level cinematic planning. WanPE formulates shot-level cinematic plans via video-grounded reverse construction and employs Semantic-Consistency GRPO (SC-GRPO) to faithfully preserve user requirements across shots and over time. To benchmark this capability, we curate WanPEval, a human-annotated testbed covering durations from 5 to 30 seconds across varying intent granularities, supported by approximately 11K blind pairwise assessments. When powering Wan3.0's video generator, WanPE-397B boosts human preference over raw user prompts by 10.66-18.84 points at 5-15 seconds and by a dramatic 50.86 points in the 30-second arena. Ablation studies show that reverse construction demonstrates clear superiority over forward rewriting, while SC-GRPO robustly preserves semantic fidelity across model scales. Ultimately, WanPE leads all evaluated commercial offerings at 5-15 seconds and remains competitive with Seedance 2.5 at 30 seconds.
